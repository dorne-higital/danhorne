Help with Dobie Dash

Email: hello@danhorne.co.uk. Or in the game, Settings → Send feedback. I reply within a couple of days.

### Common questions

How do I play? Tap to jump, tap again in the air to double jump. Eat biscuits, dodge the obstacles, and when a boss chases you, dodge its attacks and jump on its head.

I got a new phone. Where's my progress? It's saved to iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple ID and open the game; it picks up the copy with the most play. For the leaderboards, use League → Keep your place (Sign in with Apple) on the old phone first.

I bought something and it's missing. Wardrobe → Shop → Restore purchases. If that doesn't bring it back, email me the date and what you bought.

I don't want ads. Remove Ads (Shop) stops the ads between runs. Rewarded ads are always your choice.

Someone has a rude name on the leaderboard. Tap ••• next to it and Report name. Once enough people report it, it's hidden until I've checked it.

How do I add a friend? Your friend code is at the bottom of your Profile. Friends add it in League → Friends.

Delete my data. Email me your friend code and I'll delete your player and scores. See the privacy policy: danhorne.co.uk/dobie-dash/privacy
